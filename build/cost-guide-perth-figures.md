# Perth published cost figures, collected for the cost guide

**Collected 12 September 2026.** Source data for the Perth Tiling Specialists cost guide, using the Canberra method: published figures from multiple sources, tabulated, attributed by publisher type, with the disagreement explained.

**Attribution rule.** Reader-facing copy uses the publisher type column, never the business name. Peak bodies (HIA, Master Builders WA) may be named. This file keeps the real names so figures stay traceable internally.

## 4. Perth-specific cost drivers worth writing up

Each of these has a published source and none of them appear in the Canberra guide, so this material is original to this build.

| Driver | Claim | Publisher type | Real source | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Slab-on-ground plumbing | Cutting a concrete slab to relocate plumbing adds $2,000 to $6,000 per point in Perth | Perth supplier | discount.com.au | Published, single source. Attribute, do not assert |
| FIFO and mining labour scarcity | Perth labour sits 10 to 20% above Melbourne | Cost guide site | Leadkit | Single source, plausible, attribute clearly |
| Freight | Materials 8 to 15% higher than eastern states | Peak body | Master Builders WA | Strongest of the three |
| Suburb premium | Western suburbs 20 to 30% over outer suburbs | Cost guide site | Leadkit | Attribute |
| Coastal salt zone | Stainless tapware and corrosion-resistant hardware in Hillarys, Scarborough | Cost guide site | whatsthedamage.com.au | Attribute |
| Pre-1990 asbestos | Licensed removal $2,000 to $8,000 per room | Perth renovation company | House & Co | Attribute. **Pair with the WorkSafe WA rule, not the ACT rule** |
| Galvanised steel pipes | Replacement in a bathroom zone $3,000 to $7,000 | Perth renovation company | House & Co | Attribute |

**The slab-on-ground point is the best Perth-specific angle available.** Perth's housing stock is heavily slab-on-ground, so relocating a waste is materially more expensive than in a suspended-floor city. It explains why "keep the layout" is better advice in Perth than almost anywhere. Worth its own subsection.

## 5. Sources not to use

- **hipages, Airtasker, ServiceTasker, Yellow Pages** as *authorities*. They can appear in the comparison table as "tradie marketplace" figures, which is exactly the point Canberra makes about programmatic cost sites, but never as the basis for a claim.
- **HomeAdvisor** and other US sources. Per square foot, USD, wrong market. Excluded.
- **Boda Building Group** by name. It ranks #7 on `floor tiling perth`, so it is a competitor. Its figures are usable under the publisher-type rule; its name is not.
